Soul JJ ("we", "our") operates the silverwolflabs.com website and related
services (the "Services"). This policy explains what personal
information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account info:** name, email, phone number, emergency contact
- **Training info:** belt rank, check-in history, class attendance
- **Payment info:** handled by Stripe; we never see your card number
- **Technical info:** IP address, browser type, pages visited

## 2. How we use it

- To operate your membership and schedule your classes
- To process payments via Stripe
- To send you transactional emails (signup verification, receipts,
  class reminders)
- To comply with legal obligations (waiver enforcement, tax records)

## 3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share only with:
- **Stripe** (payment processing)
- **Supabase** (data hosting — US region)
- **AWS** (infrastructure, email via SES)
- Legal authorities when required by law

## 4. Your rights

You can request:

- **Access** — a copy of all data we hold about you
- **Correction** — fixing inaccurate information
- **Deletion** — removal of your account and associated data
- **Portability** — machine-readable export

Submit a request at [/privacy/request](/privacy/request). We
respond within 30 days.

## 5. Retention

- **Active member data:** for the duration of your membership + 7
  years (tax records requirement)
- **Waiver signatures:** 5 years from the date of signing
- **Marketing emails:** until you unsubscribe
